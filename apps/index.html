Add yours

Listing takes a pull request.

Add an entry to apps.json and open a pull request. It is merged when the app meets these rules, which a reviewer can check in a minute:

  • The app opens at a public URL and works with no account on any server.
  • Its chain is live: a public node serves it, and it appears in the explorer.
  • The summary says what it does in 12 words or fewer, with no superlatives or price talk.
  • It links its source, or carries the closed source tag.

A listing is a convenience, not a gate. Your app works on Lattice whether or not it is here, and its chain shows up in the explorer once a node hosting it declares a public read URL.

A listing is not an audit. Read an app’s source before you trust it with coin.
